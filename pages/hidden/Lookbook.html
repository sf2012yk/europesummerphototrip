A European Holidays

- Themes of Summer -

Their Journeys Through Europe’s Holidays and Memories

Layering the landscapes of the cities we visited with the sense of longing felt along the journey.
Rather than preserving the trip exactly as it happened, this visual travel essay reimagines an ideal holiday,
reconstructed through the perspectives of “the girls.”

It invites you on a journey to discover the true Europe—
the one that lies quietly behind the familiar glamour of its tourist destinations.
